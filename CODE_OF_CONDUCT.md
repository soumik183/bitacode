# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being direct about code without being unkind about the person who wrote it.
- Assuming good faith, and asking a question before assuming a mistake.
- Accepting that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the
  project's scope, and that "no" is a complete answer.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate reasons when appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the
maintainer via a private message on GitHub or the contact on the profile at
<https://github.com/soumik183>. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will
be respected.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: a private warning, then a
temporary ban from interaction, then a permanent ban.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, code comments — and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public.
